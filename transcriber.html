Your transcript appears here, line by line, while the recording is processed. Click a time to replay that moment, add a speaker to any line, and edit the text directly.

Machine transcription makes mistakes, especially with names, case citations and cross-talk. Check it against the recording before relying on it or filing it.

How it works

  1. Add the recordingDrop in the hearing audio or video. Long recordings are read in pieces.
  2. Pick the languageEnglish, Urdu, or Mixed. Choose Best quality for Urdu.
  3. TranscribeLines appear as it works. Stop anytime and continue later.
  4. Review and exportAdd speakers, fix names and citations, then export to Word.
The first transcription downloads a speech model once (150 MB to 1 GB). Works best in Chrome or Edge on a computer with a graphics card.
